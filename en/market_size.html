Sources and notes

- Prepared foods, seasonings and confectionery use the production index from MAFF's Food Industry Trend Survey (FY2020 = 100). The survey does not include actual volumes (tonnes) or values (producer shipment value), so they are not shown.
- Soft drinks show actual production volume and value from the free digest edition of the Japan Soft Drink Association statistics (all 8 segments combined).
- Japanese fiscal years (FY) run from April to March. Values are in Japanese yen (JPY); 1 trillion yen = 1,000,000 million yen.
- This page is intended as a reference for planning and trend spotting. For official market size figures, please consult private research reports or the official statistics of each industry association. Source documents are in Japanese.
