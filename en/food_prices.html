How to read this page

Three things to know

It's an index, not yenEvery chart uses the Consumer Price Index with the 2025 average set to 100. A value of 80 means prices are 20% below the 2025 average; 110 means 10% above.
Why compare with 2019?2019 is the last full year before COVID, the weak yen and the surge in global commodity prices. It is a common baseline for "how much more expensive is life now?"
Where the data comes fromJapan's Statistics Bureau surveys retail prices nationwide every month. This page is updated around the 25th, after each release.

Sources & notes

• Yearly values are annual averages. Values before 2025 are the Statistics Bureau's linked index, rebased to 2025 = 100.
• "vs. 2019" compares the latest monthly index with the average of the twelve monthly values of 2019.
• Engel's coefficient for 2018 could not be confirmed and is left blank. Values for 1946–2017 and 2019–2022 come from secondary sources based on the same government survey and are shown as a dashed line.
• This page is reference material. Please check the original statistics before quoting figures.
