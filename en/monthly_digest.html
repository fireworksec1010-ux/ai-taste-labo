Reading notes for international readers:
- "Price index" refers to the item-level Consumer Price Index (CPI) published by the Statistics Bureau of Japan. The September 2026 edition uses 2020 = 100; the site's price pages have since moved to the new 2025 = 100 base.
- Japanese fiscal years (FY) run from April to March. Amounts are in Japanese yen (JPY).
- Highlight figures are based on this site's data pages (Statistics Bureau of Japan, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, etc.). Pages marked "JA" are currently available in Japanese only.
- Trend items are summaries of press releases and trade publications (sources in Japanese). Please check the original sources for exact figures.
